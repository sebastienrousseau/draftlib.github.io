draft turns research papers into publication-ready Markdown where every
sentence is grounded in a quote-verified claim from the source. It needs no API
key, runs offline against a local model, and ships provenance a reader can
check.

It is built and maintained by
[Sebastien Rousseau](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau) and is free and open
source under the MIT or Apache-2.0 licence.
