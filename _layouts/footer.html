Grounded by construction. Every sentence rests on a claim whose quote appears verbatim in the source, and every number in the claim appears in that quote. The model arranges facts; it never sources them.

No API key, no network required. Online, draft writes through the AI coding-agent CLI you already have. Offline, it falls back to a local model. Your sources never leave your machine.
